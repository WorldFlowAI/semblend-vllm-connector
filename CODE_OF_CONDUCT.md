# Code Of Conduct

This project expects professional, technical, and respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Stay focused on the technical issue.
- Be clear about evidence, assumptions, and tradeoffs.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Respect maintainers' time and project scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Public disclosure of private prompts, request data, credentials, or security
  reports.
- Repeated off-topic or bad-faith participation.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
when needed to keep the project productive and safe.
